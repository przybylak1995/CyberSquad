What Do We Offer?

Long Mission

This is a long-term contract in which we are discussing a set number of testing hours per month for a minimum period of six months. By implementing this approach, our operators gain much better insight into the technologies used to build the application. Additionally, we will establish a connection that allows you to reach out to us with any questions regarding new releases, unexpected alerts, or anything else, and we will assist you throughout the entire duration of the agreement. After a testing period with several customers, this method has proven to be a more effective and improved approach.

Short Mission

A short-term mission is comparable to a penetration test, but with a focused, time-efficient approach. We dedicate a short period to thoroughly assess the web application, delivering one comprehensive report with our findings. This service is ideal when time is of the essence, such as when a project needs to go live quickly or when an audit is required to ensure compliance with security standards. We aim to begin the mission within a week, which is a fast turnaround in an industry where longer wait times are common.
